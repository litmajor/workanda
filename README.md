
Workanda 🚀

The Future of Freelancing – Secure, Transparent, and Empowering


🌍 Work Anywhere, Get Paid Securely

Workanda is a next-generation freelance marketplace that connects clients with skilled freelancers, ensuring secure payments through escrow and providing a seamless experience with multi-currency support, Web3 integration, and AI-driven trust scores.


🔥 Why Workanda?

✅ Safe & Secure – Payments are held in escrow until work is completed.
✅ Fair Fees – Unlike other platforms, Workanda keeps costs low for freelancers.
✅ Multi-Currency & Crypto Payments – Get paid in fiat or crypto.
✅ AI-Powered Trust Score – Prevents scams and builds credibility.
✅ Seamless Collaboration – Integrated task management & milestone tracking.
✅ Telegram Bot Support – Get instant job alerts and updates.


🚀 How It Works

1️⃣ Clients post a job – Describe the work and set a budget.
2️⃣ Freelancers bid – Talented professionals submit proposals.
3️⃣ Escrow protects both parties – Payment is held securely.
4️⃣ Work gets done & reviewed – Track progress with milestones.
5️⃣ Payment is released – Client approves & freelancer gets paid.

💡 No more delayed payments or disputes – Workanda ensures fairness for everyone!
